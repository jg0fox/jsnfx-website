Dark Dark Dark · Star Scrub

Interaction experiments — reimagining date selection as navigating a field of stars. A curated selection.

  • Z-Travel + Focus
    True perspective. Drag to pan, ⌘+scroll to fly forward/back through depth, click a star to travel to it.
  • Real Calendar
    Anchored to real dates. Depth cues (dimming, tint, glow) + hover preview + a live wayfinding readout.
  • Lens HUD
    A central focal circle. Stars stay wild outside it; inside, the calendar reveals itself — hour labels, day headers, month + year.
  • Zoom + Lens
    Dense hours-field + lens, navigated by anisotropic ⌘+zoom over a continuous timeline. Coarsens to months, years, decades at the extreme.
  • Scheduling
    The scheduling interface. Click stars to select hours, shift-drag to lasso a block, review in the side panel, and Create. T returns to today.
  • Mobile Flip
    Touch-first. Flip the card to toggle Browse ⇄ Select. Best in a narrow / device-emulated window.
