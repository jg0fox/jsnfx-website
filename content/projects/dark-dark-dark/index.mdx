Dark Dark Dark is a single-use scheduler. No accounts required. You just fly through the stars, set your availability, share a link, and a guest picks a time.

People have a rock-solid mental model of calendars and scheduling. It's so well-defined that I wanted to see if I could find some room to make a mundane task feel wondersome.

Education through entertainment is something that really floats my boat.

![The scheduling interface, where a lens resolves the familiar calendar out of the star field.](/projects/dark-dark-dark/scheduling.png)

The idea arrived through a series of interaction experiments, a spectrum of abstraction from a raw, wild star field down to a working scheduling interface. A selection to explore:

- [Z-Travel + Focus](/explorations/dark-dark-dark/experiments/03-ztravel-focus.html). True perspective. Fly forward and back through depth, and click a star to travel to it.
- [Real Calendar](/explorations/dark-dark-dark/experiments/05-real-calendar.html). Anchored to real dates, with depth cues and a live wayfinding readout.
- [Lens HUD](/explorations/dark-dark-dark/experiments/09-lens-hud.html). A focal circle where the familiar calendar resolves out of the wild star field.
- [Zoom + Lens](/explorations/dark-dark-dark/experiments/11-zoom-lens.html). Anisotropic zoom over a continuous timeline, from a day's hours out to decades.
- [Scheduling](/explorations/dark-dark-dark/experiments/12-scheduling.html). The working interface. Click stars to select hours, lasso a block, and create.
- [Mobile Flip](/explorations/dark-dark-dark/experiments/13-mobile-flip.html). Touch-first. Flip the card to toggle browse and select.
