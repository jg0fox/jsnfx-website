Dark Dark Dark is a dead-simple, single-use scheduler. No accounts — you set your availability, share a link, and a guest picks a time.

People have a rock-solid mental model of calendars and scheduling. It's so well-defined that there was room to experiment: to treat browsing through dates as moving through a field of stars. Reframing a mundane task this way lends it a little persuasion, enticement, and delight.

That instinct translates directly to visualizing research insights. Sometimes, beyond just showing the data, you need to compel people, persuade them, and entertain them. Education through entertainment is something I'm passionate about.

![The scheduling interface — a lens resolves the familiar calendar out of the star field.](/projects/dark-dark-dark/scheduling.png)

The idea arrived through a series of interaction experiments — a spectrum of abstraction from a raw, wild star field down to a working scheduling interface. You can [browse a selection of them](/explorations/dark-dark-dark/).
